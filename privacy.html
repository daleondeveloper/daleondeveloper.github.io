Privacy Policy — Thorn & Dew

Effective date: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Thorn & Dew (the “Game”), developed by Andrii Fedunets (Fedunets Games) (“we”, “us”), handles information when you play.

1. Data we collect

The Game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email or contacts. Your game progress is stored locally on your device.

To show ads, measure performance and send notifications, the Game includes third-party software development kits (SDKs) that may automatically collect:

  • Device information (model, operating system version, language, screen size);
  • Identifiers: identifier for advertisers (IDFA — only with your permission via Apple App Tracking Transparency), identifier for vendors (IDFV), and SDK-generated identifiers;
  • IP address and coarse (city-level) location derived from it;
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, rewarded views);
  • Gameplay analytics events (session length, level progression, in-game actions).

2. Third-party services we use

  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource (ad mediation) — privacy policy
  • Unity Ads (advertising) — privacy policy
  • Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) (advertising) — privacy policy
  • DT Exchange (Digital Turbine) (advertising) — privacy policy
  • Mintegral (advertising) — privacy policy
  • Google Firebase Analytics (analytics) — privacy policy
  • OneSignal (push notifications) — privacy policy
  • AppsFlyer (install attribution) — privacy policy

These providers process data as independent controllers or our processors under their own policies linked above.

3. How the data is used

  • To display ads, including rewarded ads that grant in-game bonuses;
  • To show personalized ads — only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt; otherwise ads are contextual (non-personalized);
  • To measure ad performance and attribute app installs to ad campaigns;
  • To understand aggregate gameplay behaviour and improve game balance;
  • To deliver push notifications you have agreed to receive.

4. Tracking permission (iOS)

On the first launch the Game asks for permission to track via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not used and ads remain non-personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. Push notifications

Notifications are optional. You can disable them at any time in Settings → Notifications on your device.

6. Data retention

We do not operate our own servers and do not store your personal data ourselves. Data collected by the SDK providers listed above is retained according to their policies. Game progress is kept on your device and is deleted when you delete the Game.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. The Game is rated 13+ on the App Store.

8. Your rights

Depending on your region (including GDPR in the EEA/UK and CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to or opt out of certain processing. Because the data described above is held by the SDK providers, the fastest way to exercise these rights is through the links in section 2; you can also contact us and we will help direct your request.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this address; the effective date above reflects the latest revision.
